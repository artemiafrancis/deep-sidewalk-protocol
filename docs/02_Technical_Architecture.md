# DSP Technical Architecture (Core Data Schemas v0.1)

Decentralized Electronic Passport / DeEP Sidewalk Protocol (DSP)

Official Portal: https://www.lead-project.xyz/

---

## 1. Overview
The DSP implementation is structured around four primary data schemas. Together, they establish verifiable identity, safe navigation telemetry, public contribution accounting, and automated value settlement.

1. **DSP-Passport Credential**: Machine identity, operating authorization, and active coexistence binding.
2. **DSP-Difficulty Patch**: Dynamic, non-personal environmental difficulty and local risk telemetry.
3. **DSP-Contribution Receipt**: Verifiable cryptographic proof of safe operations in shared public spaces.
4. **DSP-Value Transaction**: Settlement and automated value allocation supporting public social returns.

---

## 2. The Four Primary Schemas

### 2.1. DSP-Passport Credential
- **Role**: Serves as the digital passport for physical AI operating in public environments. Built upon W3C Verifiable Credentials standards.
- **Key Functions**:
  - Encapsulates operator, maintainer, and certifier decentralized identifiers (DIDs).
  - Explicitly states safety clearance phases (Phase 0 to Phase 3).
  - Binds the physical machine to active insurance coverage and social return commitments.

### 2.2. DSP-Difficulty Patch
- **Role**: Records non-personal traversal conditions across shared sidewalk grids using spatial indexing (e.g., Uber H3 mesh).
- **Key Functions**:
  - Quantifies dynamic risk metrics such as surface quality, physical obstacles, and biological coexistence stress.
  - Integrates differential privacy parameters to prevent pedestrian identification or surveillance.
  - Shares anonymous environmental insights across multi-vendor fleets without disclosing proprietary mapping pipelines.

### 2.3. DSP-Contribution Receipt
- **Role**: Provides cryptographic proof that a physical AI unit executed safe operations in public spaces.
- **Key Functions**:
  - Validates active adherence to the Yield Protocol (prioritizing elderly, guide dogs, wheelchairs, and children).
  - Calculates difficulty-adjusted utility based on the operational context.
  - Emits immutable session logs required for public trust, insurance audits, and ecosystem incentives.

### 2.4. DSP-Value Transaction
- **Role**: The settlement record governing fee distribution and societal value return.
- **Key Functions**:
  - Executes protocol-level value routing upon the exchange or consumption of difficulty telemetry.
  - Enforces automated allocation to designated public welfare recipients (e.g., retired guide dog foundations / Dog Pension).
  - Ensures transparent, audit-ready balance sheets across autonomous entities and partner networks.

---

## 3. Interaction Lifecycle

1. **Verify**: Before entering a municipal zone, the unit presents its `DSP-Passport Credential` to the local commons gateway.
2. **Navigate & Record**: During transit, traversal friction and biological interaction metrics are summarized into privacy-preserving `DSP-Difficulty Patches`.
3. **Attest**: Safe operations and respectful yielding events trigger the issuance of signed `DSP-Contribution Receipts`.
4. **Settle**: Operational rewards and data licensing fees are processed via `DSP-Value Transactions`, with mandatory social return splits routed programmatically.
