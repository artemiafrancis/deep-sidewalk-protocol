# OSP Stewardship Model (v0.1)

Public Governance, Human-in-the-Loop Oversight, and Commons Administration.

## 1. Governance Principles
Open Sidewalk Protocol (OSP) operates as an open-source, public-commons infrastructure rather than a rent-extracting corporate intermediary[cite: 3]. 

To maintain alignment between physical AI operations and human communities, the protocol enforces three non-negotiable governance principles[cite: 3]:
1. Public Benefit Invariance: The baseline social return allocation (e.g., funding biological care / guide dog support) cannot be dismantled, redirected, or diluted by algorithmic optimization[cite: 3].
2. Explicit Stewardship Boundaries: Protocol governance separates daily operational execution (handled autonomously by agents and fleets) from foundational policy changes (restricted to accountable human stewards)[cite: 3].
3. Decentralized Multi-Stakeholder Oversight: Stewardship authority is distributed across municipal liaisons, safety certifiers, welfare organizations, and operator consortiums[cite: 3].

---

## 2. Stewardship Structure

1. Human Stewards (Oversight Layer)
   - Public trusts, municipal liaisons, and animal welfare organizations[cite: 3].
   - Hold constitutional governance keys and multi-signature authorization[cite: 3].

2. AI Agent Safety Gateway (Enforcement Layer)
   - Programmatic policy checks and real-time transaction threshold gates[cite: 3].
   - Autonomous operational execution within approved boundaries[cite: 3].

3. Physical AI Fleets (Operational Layer)
   - Autonomous delivery robots, assistive mobility units, and service AMRs operating in shared spaces[cite: 3].

### 2.1. Operational Roles & Permissions
- Multi-Signature Governance: High-impact administrative functions (registry updates, safety gateway parameter thresholds, fee adjustments) require a multi-signature consensus among designated stewards[cite: 3].
- Active Care Slot Management: Stewards verify and audit eligible public beneficiaries before onboarding them to the protocol's automated allocation pipeline[cite: 3].
- Incident Intervention: In the event of catastrophic spatial conflicts or systematic telemetry manipulation, stewards hold the authority to enforce temporary municipal mesh throttling[cite: 3].

---

## 3. Human-in-the-Loop Safeguards
While autonomous AI agents manage difficulty patches, session routing, and micro-settlements, the Stewardship Model mandates active Human-in-the-Loop (HITL) checkpoints[cite: 3]:
- Emergency Circuit Breaking: Authorized certifiers or municipal operators can issue emergency stop declarations for specific geographic meshes[cite: 3].
- Audit Logging and Dispute Resolution: Disputed contribution receipts, flagged near-miss incidents, and penalty appeals are routed to open public resolution ledgers[cite: 3].
- Stewardship Transparency: All cryptographic key actions, voting records, and administrative transactions are immutably logged on-chain[cite: 3].

---

## 4. Institutional Sunset Provision
OSP stewardship is designed with an explicit exit condition: as municipal road authorities and international standards bodies (ISO/IEEE) formalize native coexistence and social return mechanisms, steward governance authority progressively decentralizes into public civil infrastructure[cite: 3].
