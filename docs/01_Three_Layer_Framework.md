# OSP Three-Layer Framework (v4.1)

Safe Coexistence, Transparent Contribution, and Automated Social Return for Physical AI.

Official Portal: https://www.lead-project.xyz/

---

## 1. Background and Structural Shift
Previous robotic paradigms prioritized closed, single-vendor deployment, leading to fragmented sidewalk access, regulatory bottlenecks, and societal friction. The Open Sidewalk Protocol (OSP) replaces monolithic systems with an open-source, modular portal infrastructure.

By decoupling operational standards from specific hardware manufacturers, OSP establishes an interoperable foundation accessible to all physical AI units, autonomous delivery systems, and assistive robotics operating in shared human spaces.

---

## 2. The Three-Layer Architecture
OSP operates through three distinct layers, balancing regulatory clarity, economic viability, and durable societal alignment:

1. Public Commons Layer (Interface & Safety)
   - Standardized navigation passport and identification
   - Verified traversal credentials for regulatory trust

2. Business and Operational Layer (Coordination)
   - Non-personal environmental difficulty patches
   - Dynamic sidewalk routing and congestion management

3. Core Social Return Layer (Alignment & Commons)
   - Cryptographic Proof of Contribution (Receipts)
   - Automated value routing to public welfare and commons

---

### Layer 1: Public Commons Layer (Safety and Governance)
- Role: Provides a clean, transparent interface for municipal authorities, regulatory bodies, and local communities.
- Mechanism: Issues standardized credentials certifying that a physical AI unit adheres to shared sidewalk etiquette, speed restrictions, and fail-safe protocols.

### Layer 2: Business and Operational Layer (Coexistence and Efficiency)
- Role: Enables multi-vendor coordination across shared last-mile environments.
- Mechanism: Ingests and shares aggregated, privacy-preserving "Difficulty Patches" (spatial risk indicators, surface conditions, temporary obstacles) without exposing proprietary mapping or user data.

### Layer 3: Core Social Return Layer (Ecosystem Sustainability)
- Role: Closes the loop between physical operation and public goodwill.
- Mechanism: Every operational unit records verifiable contribution receipts. A protocol-level rule routes a baseline allocation of network activity back to public commons and animal/community welfare initiatives, turning physical operations into an engine of continuous societal contribution.

---

## 3. Guiding Principles
- Vessel, Not Stigma: Identification must not serve as an exclusionary warning label, but as an open passport verifying clear responsibility and operational trust.
- Care Beyond Utility: Long-term alignment between physical AI and human communities requires economic feedback loops that automatically support shared social goods.
