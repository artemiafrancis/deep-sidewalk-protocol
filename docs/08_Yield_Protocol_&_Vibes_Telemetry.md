## 1. Concept: Moving from Collision Avoidance to Active Yielding
Traditional industrial robotics standards treat moving entities uniformly as dynamic physical obstacles to be avoided along minimum-distance trajectories[cite: 3, 4]. 

Open Sidewalk Protocol (OSP) introduces the **Yield Protocol**, shifting navigation objectives from purely mechanical clearance to active, courteous yielding[cite: 3, 4]. Physical AI units operating in shared human spaces must prioritize the comfort, mobility rights, and dignity of biological pedestrians[cite: 3].

## 2. Priority Hierarchy (Layer 0 Care Allocation)
When traversing public sidewalks and shared pedestrian corridors, autonomous units must adhere to a strict precedence order[cite: 3]:

1. **Vulnerable Biological Life**: Guide dogs, service animals, elderly pedestrians, wheelchair users, and young children[cite: 3].
2. **Standard Pedestrians**: Commuters, runners, and casual sidewalk users[cite: 3].
3. **Assistive & Human-Driven Micro-Mobility**: Bicycles, manual carts, and mobility scooters.
4. **Commercial Autonomous AMRs & Delivery Drones**: OSP-compliant physical AI units (lowest operational priority)[cite: 3].

## 3. Yield Protocol Dynamics

### 3.1. Proactive Buffer Distances
- **Early Deceleration**: Units detect prioritized biological targets and begin smooth deceleration outside conventional industrial braking zones[cite: 3, 4].
- **Lateral Clearance**: When passing or pausing, units enforce dynamic lateral buffer zones expanded specifically for service dogs and assistive mobility users[cite: 3].
- **Safe-Stop Stance**: In constrained bottlenecks, the autonomous unit comes to a complete halt, yielding the primary path until biological actors have safely cleared the mesh[cite: 3, 4].

### 3.2. Biological Vibes Scoring (Non-Deterministic Feedback)
To prevent pure algorithmic optimization from gaming efficiency at the expense of human comfort, OSP integrates **Biological Vibes Telemetry**[cite: 3]:
- **Coexistence Stress Index (`coexistenceStressIndex`)**: Evaluates aggregate pedestrian hesitation, sudden directional shifts, or animal stress behaviors in proximity to the unit[cite: 3, 4].
- **Care Priority Score (`carePriorityScore`)**: Quantifies the real-time density of vulnerable entities within the active spatial mesh[cite: 3, 4].
- **Vibes as the Trilemma Anchor**: Within the protocol's game-theoretic triangle (Code, Economy, Vibes), biological feedback serves as the un-hackable constraint ("Scissors") that restrains over-optimized routing ("Rock") and aggressive micro-settlements ("Paper")[cite: 3].
